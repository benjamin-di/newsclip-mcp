# Roadmap

The current release focuses on a self-hosted news reader with human sign-in and
isolated users. Its supported operating-system and image architecture matrix is
stated in the README; additional platforms require browser and image acceptance.

Useful future work includes optional package-index distribution, independently
verified additional architectures, and provider-specific usability improvements
backed by reproductions. These are not prerequisites for using the published
source and container images.

Publisher behavior can change independently of Newsclip. The provider matrix
records limitations rather than promising universal access. Report synthetic
reproducers and redacted failure categories through the contribution process.

Out of scope: purchasing subscriptions, public article redistribution, automatic
credential entry, passkeys bound to a user's own device, and arbitrary browser
navigation through a sign-in link.
