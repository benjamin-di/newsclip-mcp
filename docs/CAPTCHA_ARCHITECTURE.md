# Interactive challenges

Public Newsclip keeps publisher sign-in and interactive checks with the person
in a restricted signin-window browser. The reader detects blocked, partial and
authentication pages and reports them without promoting their content.

Ordinary browser loading can pass a publisher's passive device checks; that
does not authorize credential entry or automated interaction with an active
challenge. A human connection is independent from the subsequent full-content
assessment. Keep any diagnostic captures private.
